Using the phone

Built-in apps

Eleven apps ship with the phone. Server owners can add more with custom apps.

Phone

Four tabs along the bottom: Calls (your call log, missed calls in red), Keypad, Messages and Contacts. The last two open those apps, and the same bar appears in Contacts, so you can jump between all three without going home.

  • Tap the phone icon next to any call in the log to call back.
  • The keypad shows the contact's name as soon as you type a saved number.
  • See Calls for ringing, answering and missed calls.

Messages

Tabs for All, Unread, Groups and a shortcut to Calls. Search by name or number; the tick in the header marks everything read.

  • The composer: + on the left for a photo or your current location, emoji inside the field, and the microphone on the right for voice notes.
  • Voice notes are recorded with the player's microphone (up to 20 seconds) and play back with a waveform. Without a microphone the message still sends, showing its length.
  • Shared locations set a waypoint when tapped.
  • Group chats: the owner can rename, set a picture, invite, remove people and delete the group. Members can leave. Players who were offline see the messages they missed.
  • Texting a number you've never texted just works, and the conversation is created on the first message.

Contacts

  • Add, edit and delete contacts, with a picture from a link or from your Gallery.
  • Share a contact, or your own number from My Cards, with a player standing near you. They get an offer and must accept before it's saved.
  • Call or text straight from a contact.

Camera

The phone screen becomes the viewfinder: you hold the phone up and frame the shot through it.

InputDoes
MouseAim. This is the only app where the mouse turns the camera.
ScrollZoom, 1× to 4×
Middle clickSwitch between rear camera and selfie
Right clickTake the photo

The on-screen buttons do the same. Photos go to the Gallery, without the phone's own interface in them. Needs screencapture on the server.

Gallery

  • Photos are grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, weekday, date). Open one to step through them.
  • Add by link: paste a direct image URL to save any image into your gallery.
  • Send in a message: pick a photo, then a conversation.
  • Gallery photos can be used as contact pictures, group pictures and Lifeinvader posts.
  • Each phone keeps up to Config.PhotoLimit photos (30). Taking more removes the oldest.

Bank

Two tabs: Finance and Invoices.

  • Balances come live from your framework (cash and bank). On servers without a bank account (standalone, or ox_core without its accounts module) the phone keeps its own bank balance.
  • Transfer to any phone number, on every framework. The money leaves your account first, so a failed transfer never creates money. If the receiver is offline, it's paid out the next time they open their phone. Server owners can cap a single transfer with Config.BankMaxTransfer.
  • Activity lists transfers and paid invoices, plus anything other scripts add with AddTransaction (paychecks, purchases…).
  • Bank always uses the character's money, even with someone else's SIM card in the phone.

Invoices

Other resources (police fines, mechanics, shops) can bill a player with AddInvoice. The player gets a notification with Pay and Decline, and the invoice lands in the Invoices tab:

  • Pay takes the amount from the bank account and adds a line to Activity.
  • Decline is only offered when the sender allows it. A declined invoice stays on record as unpaid and can still be paid later.
  • Invoices belong to the character, not the SIM card: a debt follows the person.

Turn the feature off with Config.BankInvoices = false.

Garage

  • Lists the vehicles your character owns, with plate, garage and engine, body and fuel condition.
  • Locate sets a waypoint to a vehicle that's out in the world.

Vehicles are read through the framework bridge: QBX player_vehicles, ESX owned_vehicles, ox_core vehicles and ND nd_vehicles. Standalone servers have no vehicle list.

Documents

Three tabs: Inbox (your documents with search and a status filter), Notifications (anything waiting for your signature, and recent activity) and Templates.

  • Writing: a rich-text editor with bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, lists, quotes and headings, plus a coloured banner per document. Saves automatically.
  • Templates: blank, investment agreement, employment contract and bill of sale.
  • Sharing: invite people from your contacts or players nearby, to read or to edit. Only the owner manages who has access. For anyone else, "delete" means leave.
  • Signing: the owner can send a document for signing. Everyone on it signs, and the moment the last person signs, the document is locked so nobody can change what was agreed.

Scripts can create a contract and send it to a player to sign with the CreateContract export, for example from a job center.

Lifeinvader

The city's social network, with its own bottom bar:

  • Feed: Following and Discover. Posts take text, a photo or a link (images and videos show inline, YouTube links play on tap). Like, comment and repost.
  • Marketplace: list items for sale with a price, browse what others are selling.
  • Search: find any account on the server and follow it.
  • Notifications: likes, comments and follows on your posts, also pushed to your phone.
  • Account: sign up with a handle, display name, picture and bio. One phone can have several accounts and switch between them.

The feed updates live as people post, without polling: each phone refreshes at most every 1.5 seconds, and only while it's open.

Calculator

A normal calculator that also works with the keyboard. % works like on a real phone (200 + 10% = 220), AC turns into backspace while you type, and the tape keeps your last 30 results. Tap one to use it again. Nothing is saved when you close it.

Settings

Covered in Controls & basics: SIM card, notifications, sounds, wallpaper and size, 24-hour clock and resetting the layout.
