Using the phone

Notifications & calls

The phone reaches you even when it's in your pocket: it peeks up to show what arrived, rings for calls, and never takes your mouse while you're busy.

Notifications

New notifications slide in under the status bar, newest on top. Each one:

  • disappears by itself after a few seconds (5 by default). Hovering over it keeps it up;
  • can be dismissed early by swiping it up or pressing its ×;
  • opens what it's about when tapped: the conversation for a text, the call in the call log for a missed call, the document for an invite.

A dismissed notification isn't gone. It stays in the notification centre until you clear it.

The notification centre

Tap the bell in the status bar. At the top is a row of quick toggles (sounds, do not disturb, airplane mode, rearrange apps, settings), and below it the list:

  • Two or more notifications in a row from the same app collapse into one card with a count. Tap it to expand, or clear the whole stack at once.
  • History switches between everything and unread only.

Red bubbles

An app with something unread (a notification, a message, a missed call) shows a red bubble on its home screen icon. Opening the app clears it.

Do not disturb

Turn it on in Settings or the notification centre and new notifications stop popping up. Calls still ring.

The peek

When a notification arrives while the phone is in your pocket, the phone peeks up from the bottom of the screen far enough to show the newest notification, then slides back down after a moment (Config.PeekDuration, 1.5 seconds by default).

  • Press M during a peek to open the phone straight on that notification, for example right into the conversation that just texted you.
  • The peek never takes your mouse, so you can keep driving or shooting while it's up.
  • Where the mouse is free (for example while testing in a browser), clicking the peeking phone opens it, and clicking the notification opens that conversation or call.

Offers: accept or decline

Anything that would put something on your phone from someone else (a shared contact, a document invite, a group chat invite) arrives as a notification with Accept and Decline buttons. Nothing is saved until you accept.

  • Offers stay on screen until you answer, and expire after two minutes.
  • The person who sent it is told if you decline.
  • If the other player is offline, there's nobody to ask: group invites and document invites are added directly, so they're waiting next time they open the phone.

Calls

Making a call

Open the Phone app and dial from Keypad, Calls (your call log) or Contacts. The screen says Ringing… until the other person answers, and only then are you connected. If they decline, or their phone is unavailable, you're told which.

Receiving a call

An incoming call doesn't open your phone or take your mouse. The phone peeks up with the caller and two keys:

KeyDoes
YAnswer
NDecline, or hang up a call in progress
MOpen the phone properly

If the phone is already open, the call rings full screen instead.

During a call

  • Both of you are put on the same voice channel (pma-voice), so you can hear each other wherever you are.
  • Mute takes you off the channel until you unmute.
  • The call keeps going while you use other apps: it shrinks to a green timer in the status bar. Pocket the phone and it drops to call mode, a thin strip at the bottom of the screen that stays until the call ends.
  • Your character holds the phone to their ear for the whole call.

Missed calls

If the caller hangs up before you answer, you get a Missed call notification showing the caller's contact name (or number). Tap it, or press M while it peeks, and the call log opens with that call highlighted. Declining a call yourself doesn't count as missed.
