Using the phone

SIM cards

With SIM cards on, the phone is just hardware. The number, and everything that comes with it, belongs to the card.

How it works

When Config.RequireSim = true (the default, ox_inventory only), these all belong to the SIM card, not the character:

  • the phone number;
  • contacts, messages, group chats and the call log;
  • photos, documents and Lifeinvader accounts;
  • the home screen layout, wallpaper and settings;
  • anything custom apps store with SetAppData.

Each card carries a serial number in its item metadata (like SIM-4821-0093), and everything above is saved under that serial. Wherever the card goes, the data goes with it.

That's the point of the feature

Put someone else's SIM in your phone and you're reading their messages. Steal a dealer's card and you have their contacts. Settings › SIM Card shows whose card it is and says Borrowed when it isn't yours. Texts you send from a stolen card still go out under your own name: the card is the account, not the identity.

What stays with the character

Money and debts. The Bank app always shows the character's own accounts and invoices, and transfers come out of the character's money. A stolen SIM never hands over someone's bank account, and an unpaid fine stays with the person who got it.

Getting a number

  1. Get a blank SIM card

    Sell simcard items in a shop, give them out on spawn, whatever fits your server. A new card is blank.

  2. Put it in a phone

    Open the phone item in your inventory (Open SIM tray) and drop the card in the slot. A card loose in your pockets also works if your inventory has no item containers.

  3. Open the phone

    The first time a blank card is used, it registers itself to you: it gets a serial and a fresh number, and the item's description shows Number: 555-0134. From then on that card is that number.

Setup notes for server owners

  • Add both items from ox_inventory_items.lua (see Installation). The phone won't open without a SIM item to find.
  • Never let SIM cards stack. Each card has its own identity in metadata, and stacking would merge two numbers into one slot. The provided definition already sets stack = false.
  • Whitelist the tray. Give the phone's container whitelist = { 'simcard' }, or players can store anything in the single slot.
  • Item names must match Config.ItemName and Config.SimItem.

Turning SIM cards off

Set Config.RequireSim = false and everything belongs to the character instead. Each character keeps one number for life. This also happens automatically if ox_inventory isn't running.

Switching on a live server

Data saved without SIMs belongs to characters, and data saved with SIMs belongs to cards. Changing RequireSim on a server with existing players gives everyone an empty phone. The old data is still in the database, but it isn't migrated. Decide before launch.
