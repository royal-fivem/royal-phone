Using the phone

Controls & basics

How players open the phone, get around it and make it their own. Good to share with your community.

Controls

Every key can be changed by the player in Settings › Key Bindings › FiveM (look for "Royal Phone"). Server owners set the defaults in config.lua.

When the phone won't open

  • You don't have a phone item, or there's no SIM card in it (on servers that use items).
  • You're dead, cuffed or swimming.

Moving with the phone open

You can walk, run, jump and drive with the phone up. Two things are different while it's open:

  • The camera doesn't turn. Your mouse controls the phone, so moving it can't also spin your view. Close the phone to look around. The Camera app is the exception: there, the mouse aims the lens.
  • Typing never moves you. While a text field is focused, all game controls pause, so typing "was" in a message doesn't send your character walking. They come back as soon as you click out of the field.

Weapons, melee and the pause menu are blocked while the phone is open. Your character holds the phone in front of them, and to their ear during a call.

The three positions

The phone isn't just open or closed. When it's in your pocket it can still rise a little to show you something, without taking your mouse:

PositionWhat you seeWhen
OpenThe whole phone, bottom-right of the screen. Mouse controls the phone.You pressed M.
PeekThe top of the phone with the newest notification or the incoming call card.A notification or call arrives while it's in your pocket.
Call modeJust the status bar with the call timer.You're on a call with the phone pocketed.

In peek and call mode the game keeps your mouse, so you can keep driving or shooting. Press M during a peek to open the phone straight on that notification. More in Notifications & calls.

The home screen

  • Four apps in the dock along the bottom: Phone, Messages, Contacts and Camera by default.
  • Pages of 4 × 5 apps. A new page appears once the first is full, or when you drag an icon past the right edge.
  • Red bubbles on an icon mean that app has something unread. Opening the app clears it.

Rearranging apps

  1. Hold any icon until the icons start to wiggle (or use Rearrange apps in the notification centre).
  2. Drag it where you want it, including into or out of the dock.
  3. Hold it against the right edge to move to the next page, or the left edge to go back.
  4. Tap empty space (or press Esc) when you're done. Empty pages are removed automatically.

To start over, use Settings › Privacy › Reset layout.

Flip-out mode

The map icon in the top-right corner unfolds the phone to three times its width, like a foldable. Apps get more room and the home screen shows more icons per row. Tap it again to fold it back. Your layout isn't changed by folding.

Wallpaper links

The link must point at the image file itself (ending in .png, .jpg, .webp…), not at the web page that shows it. On Imgur, right-click the image and choose "Copy image address".

What's saved, and where

The home screen layout, wallpaper, size and settings are saved on the server, per phone. With SIM cards on, they belong to the SIM, so they come with the card when it goes in another phone. Without SIM cards they belong to the character.
